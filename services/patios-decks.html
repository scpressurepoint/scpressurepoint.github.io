Student-owned and operated in Columbia, SC. Text a photo for a fast quote — (803) 272-8118.

Home / Services / Patios & Decks

Back patios and pool decks stay wet longer in Midlands humidity, which feeds algae and makes surfaces slippery. We refresh concrete, pavers, brick, and stone patios — and use wood-safe methods when a deck needs cleaning without splintering or furring the grain.

How we do it

  1. 1
    Layout & furniture

    We confirm what moves off the patio and note paver sand, wood type, or sealed surfaces.

  2. 2
    Pre-treatment

    Organic growth and tannin stains get appropriate dwell time before agitation.

  3. 3
    Surface-appropriate wash

    Concrete and pavers get surface cleaning; wood decks get lower pressure and wood-safe chemistry when applicable.

  4. 4
    Rinse & inspect

    We rinse toward drainage, reset furniture if agreed, and walk the space with you when you are home.

Pricing: Most back patios: about $100–$320 depending on size and condition. See full pricing guide.
