Home / Services / Driveways

Columbia humidity and red clay leave dark algae lines and oil stains on driveways fast. SC Pressure Point restores concrete and asphalt with a measured process: pre-treat, agitate where needed, and rinse without etching the surface.

How we do it

  1. 1
    Walk-through & quote

    We measure or estimate square footage, note oil stains, expansion joints, and drainage. You get a per-sq-ft price before we start.

  2. 2
    Pre-treatment

    Cleaning solution is applied to break down organic growth, tire marks, and embedded grime. Heavily stained areas get extra dwell time.

  3. 3
    Surface cleaning

    A surface cleaner or appropriate nozzle pattern removes buildup evenly. We adjust pressure for aged or porous concrete.

  4. 4
    Detail & rinse

    Edges, aprons, and garage transitions are finished by hand. We rinse toward drains and confirm even color before we leave.

Pricing: Most driveways: $0.20–$0.40/sq ft. Typical two-car driveway: about $125–$275. See full pricing guide.
