Home / Services / Steps & Entryways

Entry steps collect pollen, mildew, and foot traffic grime faster than flat concrete. We treat brick, concrete, and stone steps with the right pressure and chemistry so mortar and treads stay intact while the entry looks sharp again.

How we do it

  1. 1
    Material assessment

    Brick, concrete, and natural stone each get a different pressure and chemical plan.

  2. 2
    Pre-soak & apply

    Steps are pre-wet; cleaning solution loosens mildew and ground-in dirt in treads and risers.

  3. 3
    Controlled cleaning

    We work top-to-bottom with fan tips or soft wash methods on delicate brick and mortar.

  4. 4
    Safety check

    Before we leave, steps are rinsed free of residue and checked for even color on all treads.
