Fence Pressure Washing

Student-owned and operated in Columbia, SC. Text a photo for a fast quote — (803) 272-8118.

Home / Services / Fences

Fence lines under trees pick up green mildew and black streaks that age the whole yard. We clean wood, vinyl, and aluminum fencing with pressure and chemistry matched to the material so boards and panels brighten without gouging or warping.

How we do it

  1. 1
    Material ID

    Wood, vinyl, and aluminum each need different pressure limits and cleaning approach.

  2. 2
    Pre-treat

    Mildew and organic growth are treated along rails, posts, and pickets.

  3. 3
    Even pass

    We work in consistent sections so color evens out without lap marks on long runs.

  4. 4
    Rinse & review

    Fence lines are rinsed and checked from multiple angles for missed panels.
