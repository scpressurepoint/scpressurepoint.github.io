Built on Real Accountability

SC Pressure Point is a student-owned pressure washing company based in Columbia, SC. Founded by Parker Branham, every job carries his name and his standards.

A lot of pressure washing companies around Columbia send out whoever is available and charge a premium for it. SC Pressure Point was built to offer something different: fair pricing, direct communication, and work you can actually stand behind. When you reach out, you reach the owner.

Whether Parker is on-site or leading a crew, the standard stays the same. If something falls short, he hears about it directly and makes it right personally.
